# Writing Style Guide

Copy this file to `style-guide.md` and customize.

## Voice & Tone

- **Voice:** Direct, specific, unpolished-but-professional
- **Tone:** Builder mentality, evidence-based, humble
- **Energy:** Practical bias toward shipped work over process

## What to Avoid

Clichéd phrases the generator will flag:
- "I am excited to apply"
- "Perfect fit"
- "Passionate about innovation"
- "Dynamic team"
- "Leveraging cutting-edge"
- "Dear hiring manager"
- "To whom it may concern"

## Grammar & Style

- Paragraph length: 2–3 sentences (max 4)
- Use specific examples over abstractions
- Avoid jargon unless necessary (explain if used)
- Contractions OK ("I'm", "you're")
- Active voice preferred

## Signoff

- Sign: "Best, [Your Name]"
- Keep short, no "Sincerely" or "Warmest regards"

## Example Paragraph

❌ *Generic:*
> I am excited about this opportunity to join your dynamic team and leverage my passion for innovation to drive cutting-edge solutions.

✓ *On-brand:*
> The role caught my attention because you're handling payment routing at scale—a problem where technical depth actually matters. I've shipped similar systems and can move quickly from diagnosing friction to building the first version.

## Customize These Sections

### Your Core Differentiator
*Replace with your unique selling point:*
> I can move between product framing, technical implementation, and direct communication without needing those to be separate workstreams.

### Your Proof Points
*Replace with 2–3 concrete achievements:*
- Built and shipped [concrete artifact] that improved [metric]
- Led [specific project] resulting in [measurable outcome]
- Diagnosed [technical/operational problem] and drove fix

### Your Target Environment
*Replace with what energizes you:*
> I work best in: async-first orgs, long-term ownership, data-driven decision-making, cross-functional shipping.

### Gaps You're Honest About
*Replace with real gaps + learning velocity:*
> Limited [area], but recent work forced me to learn unfamiliar domains quickly and document the learning through shipped artifacts.

## Usage

The draft tool will use these rules to generate letters that sound like you. Keep it concise and update as your positioning evolves.
